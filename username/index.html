      The theme lists leave out rude, hateful, and grown-up words, and every possible pair is checked for rude words they might spell together before a list is published.

      Make a Username With Your Name or Nickname

      Type a nickname, initials, or any short word into Include a word, and every name in the list is built around it: before a character (JunoFox) or after a describing word (CosmicJuno). Letters and digits only, up to twelve.

      The word stays on your device. It is not added to the address or the settings link, and it is cleared when you reload the page. Keep in mind that a username with your real name in it is easier to link back to you.

      Keep a Shortlist

      Tap the star beside a name to keep it. Your shortlist appears under the generator with a copy button for each name, so you can try your favorites on the sign-up page one after another. It is saved in this browser only and holds up to fifty names.

      How to Make a Username Unique

      The line under the list shows how many different names your settings can make. Each theme makes between about 8,000 and 20,000, and Mixed over 66,000. Adding a number multiplies that by 85, because the two-digit numbers skip a few that read as codes, such as 69 and 88.

      • If it is taken, keep going. Press More names, try another theme, or turn on the number.
      • Leave out your full name and birth year. They make a name easy to link back to you.
      • Say it out loud. If you will share it with friends or teammates, pick one that is easy to spell from hearing it.
      • Use a different name where you want to stay separate, such as games, forums, and marketplaces.

      Then Give It a Strong Password

      A username is public: it is the part other people see. What keeps the account yours is the password and two-step verification. Make a password for the new account, or a passphrase if you will type it by hand, and use it for that account only.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      How do I create a unique username?

      Pick a theme you like, set the maximum length for the site you are joining, and copy a name from the list. If the site says it is taken, press More names, or turn on the number for a name that is almost never taken.

      What is a good gamertag?

      Short, easy to say, and one clear image, like a describing word and a creature: SlyPanther or MegaBobcat. Names without a number look better, so try a few before adding one. The Gamer theme makes this kind of name, and the Roblox, Minecraft, and Twitch choices in the length menu make it fit.

      What is an OG username?

      “OG” means original: a very short or single-word name claimed in a platform’s early days, such as a three-letter handle. They are rare because almost all are taken, and buying or selling them breaks most platforms’ rules. A two-word name from a theme is the practical way to get one that is yours alone.

      Are five-letter usernames rare?

      On large platforms, yes: nearly every short real word was claimed years ago. For the shortest names here, choose Random words, set it to one word, and turn the number off, but expect most of them to be taken already.

      Should I use the same username everywhere?

      Only where you want people to recognize you, such as a public creator profile. Everywhere else, a different name for each site keeps your accounts from being linked together.

      Can I use the generated names for free?

      Yes. They are combinations of ordinary words, and they are yours to use. If you plan to build a brand on one, check that it is not someone else’s trademark first.

      Can I get usernames in another language?

      The themes are in English, which most sites and players use for handles. For other languages, choose Random words and pick a word list with the arrow beside it: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Catalan, Dutch, or Ukrainian and Russian written in Latin letters. Accents are removed, because most sites only accept the letters a to z in usernames.
